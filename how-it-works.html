How it works

A safety checkpoint between your CSV and your catalog.

Nothing changes at upload. Every import moves through an explicit preview and approval flow.

1

Upload supplier CSV

Select a CSV up to 10 MB and 10,000 data rows. Common comma, semicolon, tab, and pipe delimiters are detected.

2

Map columns

Choose the SKU and Supplier Cost columns. Supplier SKU and Product Name are optional context.

3

Configure pricing

Choose markup or target margin, enter the percentage, and set the price-change warning threshold.

4

Review the preview

Inspect current and proposed costs and prices. Warnings are highlighted; invalid and ambiguous rows are blocked.

5

Apply deliberately

Only an authorized administrator can apply after preview. Safe and warning rows may be applied; blocked rows are skipped.

6

Review or roll back

The result reports changed, skipped, warning, and failed rows. The latest applied import can be rolled back.

Visible feedback

The preview explains what will happen.

Each row shows the SKU, product, old and new supplier costs, cost change, current and proposed regular prices, price change, and match status.

A warning is not hidden. A price movement at or beyond your configured threshold is highlighted before apply.
