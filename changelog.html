Changelog

Release history.

Concise, user-facing changes for each CostHarbor Supplier Updates for WooCommerce version.

Version 0.1.3

September 4, 2026
  • Add downloadable catalog and blank CSV templates.
  • Add first-run guidance, required-column details, and an inline CSV example.
  • Clarify Supplier Cost storage, native WooCommerce COGS synchronization, and calculated Regular Price updates.
  • Explain blocked and warning rows in corrective language and improve directory positioning and screenshot captions.

Version 0.1.2

August 31, 2026
  • Preserve supplier costs and import/rollback history on uninstall by default.
  • Add an administrator-controlled permanent deletion option with an irreversible-action warning.
  • Reject empty, header-only, oversized, over-limit, and unterminated-quote CSV files.
  • Reject scientific notation, comma-decimal ambiguity, non-finite, and excessively large monetary values.
  • Stabilize the ±50% guardrail boundary and expand executable tests.

Version 0.1.0

August 31, 2026
  • Initial guarded CSV import workflow.
  • Simple product and variation SKU matching.
  • Markup and target margin pricing.
  • Preview guardrails, controlled apply, bounded history, and rollback.
  • Optional synchronization through WooCommerce's public COGS methods.
